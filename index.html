Order Workflow

Simple 3-Step Custom Journey

How we guide your custom packaging project from initial concept to handcrafted doorstep delivery.

1
Phase 01

Design Consultation

We discuss your aesthetic preferences—aligning on raw materials (velvet, silk, jute, macrame, metal), custom sizes, color schemes, net wraps, and artificial floral brooches.

2
Phase 02

Handcrafted Prototyping

We fabricate a physical prototype of your custom shagun box, ritual platter, or net-wrapped hamper. This allows you to verify the exact fabric borders, handle grips, and structural fit.

3
Phase 03

Handcrafted Production & Dispatch

Our design artisans execute your custom batch order. Each piece is individually inspected for flawless borders and accessory fittings before secure dispatch directly to Pune or nationwide.
